GlassNet: PyTorch 3-head MLP (waste loading · GFC recipe · 13 properties) trained on 3,503 §6.2 vitrification campaigns. Glass composition is assembled through the exact physics identity glass = WL·feed + Σ xₖ·eₖ — never predicted. Held-out scenario-family accuracy: WL MAE 0.006, recipe MAE 0.005, η₁₁₅₀ medRel 0.24%, accept/reject agreement 96.7%.

Published formulations: the published vitreous-state model set (VFT viscosity and conductivity, PCT ln[NL] response, sulfate retention, K-3 neck corrosion, phosphate separation) with Appendix-C covariance Upred margins.
