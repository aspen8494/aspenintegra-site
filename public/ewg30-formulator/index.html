Waste loading — optimizer basis (what the engine maximizes)

Kilograms of waste oxides per kilogram of glass produced. Every waste oxide is assumed to end up in the glass. This is the number the property-constraint set is keyed to, and the one that defines the acceptance limit.

Physical (GFC) loading (what the melt actually takes in)

Kilograms of waste oxides per kilogram of total as-fired feed — waste plus glass-forming chemicals, before calcining losses. The raw chemicals carry non-glass mass that never reaches the glass: boric acid sheds water of hydration, carbonates shed CO₂ (Na₂CO₃ delivers only ~0.59 of its mass as Na₂O), and the waste itself arrives wet. The flowsheet side of the mission model keys on this number.
